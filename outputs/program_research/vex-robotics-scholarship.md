## Overview

The Robotics Education & Competition (REC) Foundation (which runs VEX Robotics) offers scholarships for HS seniors continuing into STEM fields. Several different scholarships at different award levels.

## Requirements

- Active VEX Robotics competition participation (you qualify)
- HS senior status
- Transcript
- Short essay about STEM goals and robotics experience
- Some scholarships may require financial need statement
- Proof of VEX team registration (your team number)

## Essay Approach

Your essay should highlight:
- VEX Robotics leadership (captain or key contributor)
- Specific technical challenges you solved on the robot
- Competition achievements: top 150 worldwide, US Open invite
- How robotics has prepared you for a STEM college path
- Community impact: Robotics for All teaching program (directly shows giving back)

## Your Competitive Profile

Top 150 worldwide + US Open invite is the top tier of VEX competition. Most scholarship applicants compete but don't have this level of achievement. Plus: teaching younger students through Robotics for All shows leadership and community service.

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **January 2027:** Check roboticseducation.org for scholarship listing and deadlines
- **February 2027:** Draft essay; gather team registration info
- **March 2027:** Submit by deadline

## Notes

Multiple different scholarships may be available under the REC Foundation — apply to all for which you're eligible when the list opens. Also check VEX-specific scholarships at college/university pages (many schools offer additional scholarships to incoming VEX competitors).
