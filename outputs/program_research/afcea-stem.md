# AFCEA STEM Scholarships

**Eligibility:** HS seniors (primarily) or college students pursuing STEM degrees; US citizen; some programs require military family connection; check each scholarship for specific criteria
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

## Overview

AFCEA International (Armed Forces Communications and Electronics Association) is a professional association connecting government, military, and industry in the IT/cybersecurity/defense space. AFCEA runs multiple scholarship programs targeting students pursuing STEM fields with defense or national security applications. Key scholarship programs include: (1) **AFCEA STEM Teacher Scholarship** (not applicable), (2) **AFCEA Scholarship for Working Professionals** (not applicable), and (3) **AFCEA HS Scholarship** — for graduating seniors entering STEM majors. Some local AFCEA chapter scholarships are also available and less competitive. Check for the Raleigh/Triangle AFCEA chapter for any local awards.

## Requirements

- US citizenship
- Transcript with strong GPA (typically 3.5+ competitive)
- Essay on interest in national security, defense technology, or STEM
- Letters of recommendation
- Some programs require military family member affiliation — check each program carefully
- Declaration of intent to pursue STEM degree at accredited college
