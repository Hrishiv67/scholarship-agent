## Overview

UNC BEAM (Biomedical Engineering and Applied Mechanics) runs multiple HS engagement programs under its outreach umbrella at UNC Chapel Hill (Chapel Hill is ~30 minutes from Cary). Programs include summer research placements in biomedical engineering labs, HS symposia, and mentored independent projects. Students work with UNC BME faculty and graduate students on real research problems in areas like tissue engineering, biomechanics, medical devices, and computational modeling. The local proximity makes this a logistically easy option — commute from Cary, no housing cost.

## Requirements

- Personal statement describing interest in biomedical engineering or related field
- Some programs have GPA minimums (typically 3.5+)
