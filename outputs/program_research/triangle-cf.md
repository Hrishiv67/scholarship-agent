# Triangle Community Foundation Scholarships

**URL:** https://www.trianglecf.org/grants-scholarships/scholarships/
**Application Opens:** ~January 2027
**Deadline:** ~February–March 2027 (varies by individual scholarship)
**Award:** $500–$5,000 varies by scholarship; most are one-time awards
**Tier:** Accessible
**Eligibility:** NC Triangle area students (Wake, Durham, Orange, Chatham, Johnston counties); specific scholarships have additional criteria; generally for college-bound HS seniors
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

## Overview

The Triangle Community Foundation manages dozens of named scholarship funds on behalf of local donors and families, all distributed through one application portal. Scholarships range from general merit awards to highly specific ones tied to particular interests (environmental studies, community service, STEM, arts, first-generation students, etc.). Because they share one application portal, applying once can make a student eligible for multiple scholarships simultaneously. Awards are typically for one year of college tuition support.

## Requirements

- Create an account at the Triangle Community Foundation portal
- Transcript
- Short essay or essays (topics vary by scholarship but are often "describe your community service" or "describe your career goals")
- One or two letters of recommendation
- Financial need documentation for some scholarships
- Some scholarships require specific career interests (environmental, healthcare, education)

## Notes

This is a high-ROI application: one portal, multiple scholarships, local competition pool. Many Triangle CF scholarships are undersubscribed because applicants don't know about the foundation. Scholarships here are typically for college expenses, not HS programs — confirm the current-year age/grade eligibility. Some scholarships are for graduating seniors only (Class of 2028 would apply in spring 2028, not 2027). Verify which scholarships are open to current 11th graders vs. only 12th graders.
