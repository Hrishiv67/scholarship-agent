## Overview

Canada/USA Mathcamp is a 5-week summer program for mathematically talented HS students, emphasizing proof-based mathematics, number theory, combinatorics, topology, algebra, and related pure math fields. The program is deliberately different from academic math — it covers graduate-level topics in a summer camp format, emphasizing mathematical creativity and exploration over competition preparation. The qualifying quiz is the primary admission criterion: a set of challenging proof-based problems that genuinely test mathematical thinking. Students who make it past the quiz are then considered holistically. Acceptance rates vary but the quiz serves as an effective self-selection filter.

## Requirements

- **Qualifying Quiz** (the actual admission gate): typically 5–8 challenging math problems requiring proof-writing; released in February; submitted by April deadline
- Two letters of recommendation (math teachers, math competition coaches)
- Personal statement (short) on why you want to attend
- Financial aid application (separate; apply simultaneously with main app)
- No specific AMC/AIME score required, but the quiz difficulty is roughly AIME level
