## Overview

RTI International is one of the largest nonprofit research institutes in the world, headquartered in Research Triangle Park (RTP), NC — roughly 15 minutes from Cary. RTI's work spans public health, education, international development, environment, and advanced analytics/data science. HS-specific internship positions are not guaranteed every year; they appear based on project needs. RTI also runs a formal high school outreach initiative through its Science, Technology, and Society program. The proximity to Cary makes this a low-cost, no-housing-needed opportunity.

## Requirements

- Resume with research and technical skills (Python, ML, data analysis)
- Transcript
- Cover letter tailored to the specific position
- HS students may need a parent/guardian authorization form
- Positions vary: some are data analytics, some are public health survey work, some are education research
