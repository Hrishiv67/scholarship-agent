## Notes
Non-residential program at UT Austin. $4,500 tuition includes one research experience, all activities, NSC309 UT Extension course credit (3 credit hours), research poster, and t-shirt. Payment due only after position accepted. Program includes ~15-25 hrs/week in lab, Wednesday seminars, and closing research symposium. Selection based on personal essay and research interests alignment, NOT GPA, class rank, or teacher recommendations. Participants off on June 18 and July 5 for holidays.
