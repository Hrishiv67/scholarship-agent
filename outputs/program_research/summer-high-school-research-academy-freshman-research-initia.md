## Notes
Non-residential program at UT Austin. Selection based on personal essay and research interests only—NO transcripts, teacher recommendations, GPA, or class rank considered. Participants matched to labs based on interests, availability, and lab capacity; no switching allowed. Application opens mid-January 2027; first-round notifications end of April 2027; deposit due one week after notification. Payment not due until position offered and accepted. Program off June 18 and July 5 for holidays.
