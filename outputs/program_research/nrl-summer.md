## Overview

The Naval Research Laboratory in Washington DC is one of the largest corporate research laboratories in the US, spanning chemistry, physics, materials science, electronics, computer science, oceanography, and remote sensing. NRL's HS summer program (formally part of the Science and Engineering Apprenticeship Program, SEAP, administered by ASEE) places students in working research labs alongside NRL scientists. Students complete a research project and present findings at an end-of-summer symposium. The DC location requires either housing (NRL provides a housing list) or a commute arrangement.

## Requirements

- US citizenship (security clearance environment — background check required)
- Transcript (GPA typically 3.5+ competitive)
- Two letters of recommendation
- Personal statement
- Applies through ASEE SEAP portal: asee.org/seap
