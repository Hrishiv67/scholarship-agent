**URL:** https://www.jshs.org/  
**NC Regional Affiliate:** Find at jshs.org/affiliates (search "North Carolina")  
**Application Opens:** ~September 2026 (estimated)  
**Deadline:** ~November 15, 2026 (estimated — check NC affiliate site)  
**Award:** Regional: $500–$2,000; National: up to $12,000 + DoD sponsorship  
**Tier:** Competitive  
**Eligibility:** HS students grades 9-12; US citizen or permanent resident  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

## EASY WITH EXISTING WORK

Your Zenodo-published research paper (NC State hurricane ML tracking or Duke BME 3D scaffold) **qualifies directly** as the required paper. The JSHS format is:
1. Submit a written research paper (your Zenodo paper reformatted slightly to JSHS style)
2. Present it at the NC regional symposium (oral presentation, ~15 minutes)
3. Top regional finishers advance to the national symposium

No additional research needed — just reformatting the paper to JSHS submission guidelines (typically APA or their custom format).

---

## Requirements

- Original research paper (5,000–20,000 words depending on category)
- Paper must represent independent original research — collaborating with a professor is fine
- Oral presentation at the regional symposium (~12-15 minutes + Q&A)
- Abstract submission first, then full paper
- Research must be primarily done by the student

## Paper Categories

- Engineering and Technology (rocketry AI / drone controls)
- Environmental Science (hurricane ML tracking)
- Biomedical and Health Sciences (Duke 3D scaffold research)
- Computer Science and Mathematics

Choose the category that best fits your paper. Hurricane ML fits Environmental Science or Engineering.

## What a Strong Submission Looks Like

- Clear research question, hypothesis, methodology, and conclusions
- Original data collection and analysis — not a literature review
- Professional writing at near-college level
- Compelling oral presentation with slides and Q&A preparation
- Connection to real-world application or societal benefit

## Your Competitive Profile

Hurricane ML model is particularly strong for this competition — atmospheric/environmental + ML = novel and timely. The Zenodo preprint gives you credibility that most HS competitors lack.

## Notes

JSHS is sponsored by the Department of Defense (Army, Navy, Air Force) — this gives it prestige and a strong pipeline to DoD internships and scholarships. The oral presentation is judged by STEM professionals from DoD research labs, which is a great networking opportunity.
