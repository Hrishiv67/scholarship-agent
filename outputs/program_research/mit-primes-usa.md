## Overview

MIT PRIMES USA is the distance-learning version of MIT's flagship PRIMES research program, connecting HS students across the US with MIT math graduate student and faculty mentors for year-long collaborative research in pure mathematics, theoretical computer science, or computational biology. Research areas include combinatorics, graph theory, number theory, algebraic geometry, and related fields. The program runs January–December with weekly mentor meetings (virtual). Accepted students produce a research paper and present at the MIT PRIMES Conference in May. This is among the most prestigious HS math programs in the world.

## Requirements

- Strong AMC/AIME performance (typically AIME qualifier, ideally AIME score 8+)
- USAMO or USAJMO qualification is highly valued
- Completed challenging math coursework (Multivariable Calculus, Linear Algebra, or proof-based courses)
- Written solutions to a set of challenging math problems (provided in the application)
- Two letters of recommendation from math teachers/coaches
- This is NOT a program for applied math, ML, or engineering — it is pure math research
