## Notes
Program operates in four phases: Phase I (Jan 1 - Pi Day, ~Mar 14): reading period; Phase II (Mar 14 - Jun 15): active research; Phase III (Jun 15 - Aug 31): independent study; Phase IV (Sep 1 - Dec 31): write-up and conference presentation. Time commitment varies by phase (10+ hours/week in Phases I-II, flexible in III, as-needed in IV). Juniors work on individual or group projects; sophomores work on group projects.
