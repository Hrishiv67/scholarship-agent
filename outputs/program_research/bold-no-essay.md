## Overview

Bold.org is a scholarship aggregation platform that hosts hundreds of scholarships from individual donors, companies, and nonprofits. The no-essay category contains scholarships that require only a profile completion, a short response (1–3 sentences), or a simple application — no long-form essays. Many scholarships on Bold.org are "identity-based" (South Asian student scholarships, STEM student scholarships, community service scholarships, first-generation scholarships) or topic-based. The platform also runs its own Bold.org monthly scholarship drawings ($25,000 total monthly) for students who complete their profile.
