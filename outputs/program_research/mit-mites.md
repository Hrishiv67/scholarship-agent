# MIT MITES Semester (Minority Introduction to Engineering and Science)

**URL:** https://oeop.mit.edu/programs/mites
**Application Opens:** ~October 2026
**Deadline:** ~January 2027
**Award:** Free, fully funded 6-week residential program at MIT; room, board, tuition, and travel all covered
**Tier:** Elite
**Eligibility:** Rising HS seniors entering 12th grade; US citizens and permanent residents; program explicitly targets underrepresented minorities and students from underserved communities; demographic eligibility is important to verify
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

## Overview

MITES Semester (formerly MITES, formerly MITE²S) is one of the most prestigious free HS STEM programs in the US, hosted at MIT for six weeks in summer. Students take college-level courses in math, science, and engineering alongside MIT faculty and graduate students, participate in lab research, and receive substantial college admissions mentorship. The cohort is approximately 80 students nationally. MITES is considered a top-tier credential — comparable to RSI or SSP in prestige — and has a strong track record of students attending MIT and other highly selective universities.

## Requirements

- Online application through MIT OEOP
- Transcript (strong academic record; typically 3.8+ unweighted for competitive applicants)
- Standardized test scores (SAT/ACT; PSAT scores accepted for rising seniors)
- Two to three letters of recommendation
- Personal essays on background, STEM interest, and goals
- Demonstrated financial need and/or underrepresented minority background

## Your Profile Fit

Critical eligibility question: MITES explicitly prioritizes underrepresented minority students (Black, Hispanic/Latino, Native American, Pacific Islander) and first-generation college students. South Asian males are generally NOT considered underrepresented in STEM for the purposes of MITES eligibility.
