## Notes
Summer Youth Programs (SYP) offers 50+ explorations for grades 6-11, including 20+ engineering options. Three competitive scholarship pathways: (1) Engineering Scholars Program (weeklong), (2) Women in Engineering (weeklong), (3) National Summer Transportation Institute (two-week residential). Standard exploration courses require $150 non-refundable deposit; pathway programs require no deposit. Registration via CampDoc. Student is eligible as rising junior (11th grade male).
