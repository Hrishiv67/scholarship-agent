## Overview

The Elks Most Valuable Student (MVS) Scholarship is one of the largest merit-based scholarship programs in the United States, awarding 500 four-year scholarships annually totaling over $2.5 million. It is administered by the Elks National Foundation and open to any U.S. citizen high school senior — no Elks family affiliation required. Applicants are evaluated on three criteria: scholarship (academic achievement), leadership, and financial need. Men and women compete in separate categories, and the top 20 winners from each gender pool are invited to a Leadership Weekend in Chicago.

## What a Strong Application Looks Like

The three judging pillars are weighted equally: academic excellence (GPA, class rank, test scores), demonstrated leadership with measurable community impact, and financial need. The top 20 winners tend to combine near-perfect academic records with extraordinary leadership narratives — founding organizations, creating lasting community impact at scale, and showing initiative beyond typical extracurriculars. The financial need component means applicants from higher-income households face a structural disadvantage in the top-20 competition, though runners-up awards are still very achievable on scholarship and leadership alone. Leadership stories with quantifiable outcomes (people served, dollars raised, records set) are far stronger than vague involvement claims.
