**Application Opens:** Early September (estimated)
**Deadline:** ~October 15 (estimated; verify at URL above)
**Award:** All-expenses campus visit to Evanston, IL (flights, hotel, meals)
**Tier:** Competitive
**Eligibility:** Underrepresented high school juniors; first-generation college students and students from underrepresented backgrounds in higher education
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

Northwestern Frontiers is a multi-day fly-in program hosted by Northwestern University's Office of Undergraduate Admission. Participants spend approximately two days on campus attending academic sessions, meeting faculty and current students, sitting in on classes, and exploring residential life. The program is designed to lower financial barriers to campus visits for students who may not otherwise be able to travel to Evanston. Northwestern has top-ranked programs in engineering (McCormick School), CS, and biomedical sciences, making it highly relevant for STEM-focused applicants.

## Eligibility Note — South Asian Applicants

Northwestern's fly-in programs historically define "underrepresented" broadly to include first-generation college students, students from low-income families, and students from groups underrepresented in higher education. South Asian students are generally **not** considered underrepresented in the same way as Black, Hispanic/Latino, Native American, or Pacific Islander students, but first-generation status or low-income background can independently qualify applicants. **Hrishiv should read the eligibility page carefully before applying.** If the program restricts eligibility by race/ethnicity to specific groups that exclude South Asian, it is not worth applying. If it uses a broader definition (underrepresented in higher education, first-gen, or socioeconomic), there may be a path.

## Requirements

Typical application components include:
- Short-answer responses or brief essays (often 200–300 words each)
- Unofficial transcript or self-reported GPA
- Current high school enrollment verification (junior year)
- Teacher or counselor recommendation (sometimes optional)
- Contact information for parent/guardian

## What to Highlight in Application

- **TARC 2nd nationally** — directly relevant to Northwestern's McCormick School of Engineering
- **VEX Robotics top 150 worldwide** — demonstrates technical depth and competitive achievement
- **NC State hurricane ML research (published)** — publishable research as a 10th grader is exceptional
- **Duke BME research** — shows genuine interest in biomedical at a research institution
