# Army Science and Engineering Apprenticeship Program (SEAP)

**URL:** https://seap.asee.org/  
**Application Opens:** ~October 2026 (estimated)  
**Deadline:** ~January 15, 2027 (estimated)  
**Award:** Paid 8-week summer internship at Army research lab; $3,000–$4,000 stipend; lab depends on match  
**Tier:** Competitive  
**Eligibility:** HS students; US citizen; 3.0+ GPA; 16+ years old  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

SEAP places HS students in US Army research labs for 8 weeks each summer. Managed by ASEE (American Society for Engineering Education). Students work directly with Army scientists on real research projects — no busy work. Army labs are located across the US; you specify lab preferences.

---

## Requirements

- Application at seap.asee.org
- Official transcript (3.0+ GPA required)
- US citizenship required
- Short essays about interest in science/engineering
- List of preferred Army labs (apply to multiple for best chance)

## Army Labs That Fit Your Profile

- **ARL (Army Research Laboratory) — Aberdeen, MD:** Robotics, AI, autonomous systems
- **DEVCOM Chemical and Biological Center — Aberdeen, MD:** Biomedical, chemistry
- **Engineer Research and Development Center (ERDC) — Vicksburg, MS or various:** Environmental, atmospheric
- **CCDC Aviation and Missile Center — Redstone Arsenal, AL:** Rocketry, aerodynamics — direct fit
- **Army Corps of Engineers — various locations:** Environmental science, hydrology

CCDC Aviation and Missile Center (Redstone Arsenal) is your best profile match — TARC rocketry + aerodynamics + Python.

## What a Strong Application Looks Like

- Genuine interest in Army research (not just "any paid internship")
- Specific preferred labs with reasons why those labs
- Research experience or relevant coursework
- US citizenship and clean background check

## Your Competitive Profile

TARC 2nd nationally + ML rocketry altitude prediction makes you a strong candidate for missile/aviation labs. Python + aerodynamics experience directly relevant.

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **October 2026:** Application opens at seap.asee.org; apply immediately to 3-4 labs
- **January 15, 2027:** Deadline — submit well before
- **February–April 2027:** Labs contact candidates for matches
- **June–August 2027:** Program runs (8 weeks)

## Notes

Security clearance background check required — US citizenship is strictly enforced. Housing not provided; you need to arrange travel/housing near the lab. The $3,000–$4,000 stipend does not fully cover housing in some high-cost areas.
