## Notes
Eight-week summer internship (possible extension up to two additional weeks) at Department of Navy laboratories. Competitive program limited to about 300 placements across 38 laboratories. Student is currently eligible (rising junior, 11th grade, U.S. citizen at Green Hope High School). Individual labs may make exceptions to grade/age minimums and citizenship requirements.
