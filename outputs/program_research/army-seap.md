# Navy SEAP High School Internship

**URL:** https://www.navalsteminterns.us/internships/seap/
**Track:** Engineering
**Deadline:** Not stated on official page (not confirmed)
**Deadline quote:** Apply by the Deadline (Sept. 15 – Nov. 30)
**Opens:** Not stated on official page
**Award:** Award and Non-Award Letters are Sent Out (January-March). New participants receive $4,000 stipend; Returning participants receive $4,500 stipend, paid bi-weekly via direct deposit. Internship duration is eight weeks with possible extension up to two additional weeks.
**Eligibility:** High school students who have completed at least Grade 9, currently enrolled in high school, must be 16 years old or older by internship start date, must be a U.S. citizen (with possible lab-specific exceptions for permanent residents and dual citizens)
**Status:** eligible
**Last verified:** 2026-11-09
**Confidence:** high

## Notes
Competitive program limited to about 300 placements across 38 laboratories. Selection based on academic achievement, personal statements, recommendations, and career/research interests. Internship takes place during summer. Student is eligible (Class of 2028 = rising junior/11th grade; U.S. citizen; meets age requirement given DOB unknown but typical for 11th graders). Some labs may make exceptions to eligibility criteria.
