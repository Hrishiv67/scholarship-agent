# Navy SEAP High School Internship

**URL:** https://www.navalsteminterns.us/internships/seap/
**Track:** Engineering
**Deadline:** Not stated on official page (not confirmed)
**Deadline quote:** Apply by the Deadline (Sept. 15 – Nov. 30)
**Opens:** Not stated on official page
**Award:** New participants receive $4,000 stipend; Returning participants receive $4,500 stipend. Paid bi-weekly via direct deposit. Award and Non-Award Letters sent January-March. About 300 placements across 38 laboratories.
**Eligibility:** High school students who have completed at least Grade 9, currently enrolled in high school, must be 16 years old or older by internship start date, must be U.S. citizen (see individual lab exceptions)

## Notes
Eight-week summer internship (possible extension up to two additional weeks) at Department of Navy laboratories. Competitive program. Individual labs may make exceptions for grade minimum, age minimum, and permanent residents/dual citizens. Student is rising junior (11th grade), U.S. citizen, and appears eligible assuming age requirement met.
