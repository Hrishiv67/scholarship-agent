## Notes
This is a guide/resource document, not a specific internship program. It provides general advice on finding internships and lists 20+ organizations in the NYC area that offer high school internship opportunities (Alley Pond Environmental Center, American Museum of Natural History, Brooklyn Botanical Garden, Central Park Conservancy, etc.). The guide notes that summer internship applications are 'usually due around January' and recommends starting search 2-3 months ahead, but no specific 2026-2028 cycle dates are provided. Queens Botanical Garden is noted as offering 'year-long paid internship.' The guide emphasizes that many environmental nonprofit internships are unpaid due to budget constraints.
