## Overview

SAS Institute, headquartered in Cary NC (literally minutes from Green Hope HS), is one of the world's largest private software companies, specializing in analytics, AI, and data management. SAS runs a robust college internship program year-round and occasionally opens HS-level positions, particularly in data analytics, software testing, and technical support roles. The company also partners with local schools through its corporate citizenship initiatives. SAS is known for an exceptional campus culture, generous employee perks, and a strong pipeline to full-time employment — making an HS internship here a meaningful credential.
