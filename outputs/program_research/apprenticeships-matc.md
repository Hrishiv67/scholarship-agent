## Notes
MATC offers apprenticeships across multiple pathways: Business (LEAD), Creative Arts & Media, Community & Human Services (SERVE), Health, Manufacturing/Construction/Transportation (MCT), and STEM. For construction trades, applicants apply to local committees; for other pathways, applicants apply directly to employers. No specific 2026–2028 cycle dates found on page. Student is rising junior (11th grade) and would be eligible to pursue apprenticeships that accept high school students.
