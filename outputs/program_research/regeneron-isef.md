# Regeneron ISEF (International Science and Engineering Fair)

**URL:** https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/  
**Path:** Qualify through Triangle Science Fair (trianglesciencefair.org) → advance to ISEF  
**Triangle Science Fair Deadline:** ~January 2027 (check trianglesciencefair.org in September)  
**ISEF Award:** Up to $75,000 grand prize; category awards; sponsor prizes  
**Tier:** Elite  
**Eligibility:** HS students grades 9-12; must win at an ISEF-affiliated regional fair first  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## How ISEF Works

ISEF requires a two-step path — you cannot apply directly:
1. **Register with Triangle Science Fair** (the local ISEF affiliate) at trianglesciencefair.org
2. **Submit a project** at the Triangle fair (typically held February–March)
3. **Win an award** at Triangle Science Fair that qualifies for ISEF advancement
4. **Attend Regeneron ISEF** (national fair, late May — held in different cities each year)

This is the critical distinction — Triangle Science Fair registration is the actual first step.

## Triangle Science Fair Requirements

- Online registration (trianglesciencefair.org)
- Abstract submission
- Completed research paper / notebook
- Research plan and safety review (if lab-based)
- ISEF forms (multiple — start early, they take time)
- Display board for the fair

## What a Strong ISEF Project Looks Like

- Original hypothesis tested with real data
- Clear methodology that others could replicate
- Surprising or significant findings
- Connection to real-world impact
- Student did the actual analysis (not just assisted)

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **September 2026:** Register with Triangle Science Fair at trianglesciencefair.org
- **October–December 2026:** Finalize project; complete ISEF safety forms
- **January 2027:** Submit registration + abstract to Triangle Science Fair
- **February–March 2027:** Attend and present at Triangle Science Fair
- **May 2027:** Attend Regeneron ISEF (if you advance)

## Notes

The ISEF forms and safety review process is bureaucratic and takes longer than expected — start in October. Do NOT wait until January to start on the paperwork. Students who advance to ISEF national are highly competitive for college admissions and separate awards (Grand Award + 50+ special awards from corporations and organizations).
