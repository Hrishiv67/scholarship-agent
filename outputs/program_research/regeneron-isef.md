**Award:** Regeneron ISEF finalists compete for over $7 million in awards, prizes and scholarships across 22 different scientific categories. More than 45 professional organizations give out Special Awards at Regeneron ISEF.
**Eligibility:** Grades 9-12 students must compete in a Regeneron ISEF-affiliated science fair in their area and win the right to attend Regeneron ISEF. Students may present work with no more than 12 months of continuous research, and research cannot be performed more than 18 months before the ISEF competition they will attend.

## Notes
This is the international finals competition. Students do not enter directly—they must first compete at a local/regional/state affiliated fair. The 2026 ISEF took place May 9-15 in Phoenix. The 2027 ISEF will be held in Los Angeles, California in May 2027. Affiliated fairs conclude by mid-April each year. Student should search for affiliated fairs in North Carolina (Cary area) to begin competition pathway.
