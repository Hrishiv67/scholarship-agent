# Davidson Fellows Scholarship

**URL:** https://www.davidsongifted.org/gifted-programs/fellows-scholarship/
**Track:** Engineering
**Deadline:** 2027-02-17 (confirmed from official page quote)
**Deadline quote:** The deadline for receipt of all application materials is February 17, 2027, 11:59pm Pacific Time.
**Opens:** Not stated on official page
**Award:** $100,000, $50,000, and $25,000 scholarships awarded. Travel expenses and lodging to Washington, DC awards reception (September) provided by the Institute. Scholarship money split evenly for two-person teams.
**Eligibility:** U.S. citizens or Permanent Residents residing in the United States (or stationed overseas on active military duty), age 18 or younger as of the application deadline, who have completed a significant piece of work in one of eight categories (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics, Literature, Music, Philosophy, Outside the Box). No minimum age.
**Status:** eligible
**Last verified:** 2026-11-09
**Confidence:** high

## Notes
Two-person teams eligible; both members must meet eligibility requirements and attend events. Teams must email DavidsonFellows@DavidsonGifted.org before starting application. Community service projects and descriptions of future majors/occupations NOT accepted. Google products (Docs, Videos) NOT accepted. 2027 application cycle currently open; information session scheduled for November 12, 2026 at 4pm Pacific Time.
