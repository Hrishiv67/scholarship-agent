# Davidson Fellows Scholarship

**URL:** https://www.davidsongifted.org/fellows-scholarship/  
**Application Opens:** ~January 2027 (estimated)  
**Deadline:** ~February 15, 2027 (estimated — check davidsongifted.org in January)  
**Award:** $10,000, $25,000, or $50,000 scholarship; recognition in Washington DC  
**Tier:** Elite (very selective — profoundly gifted students with extraordinary work)  
**Eligibility:** Students under 18 at time of application; must have completed a significant work in STEM, literature, music, philosophy, or outside the box category  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

Davidson Fellows are selected for having completed a significant piece of work of merit and value to society. This is not a GPA or test score scholarship — it's for students who have done something exceptional. The bar is very high: the Davidson Institute serves profoundly gifted students.

## Your Qualifying Work Options

**Option A — Rocketry AI Altitude Prediction Model (TARC)**
- Context: Built an AI model to predict altitude in real-time during TARC rocket flights
- Achievement: Team placed 2nd nationally using this model
- Significance: Demonstrates novel application of ML to aerospace engineering

**Option B — Hurricane Track Prediction ML Model (NC State)**
- Context: ML model predicting hurricane tracks using historical meteorological data
- Published on Zenodo (research preprint)
- Significance: Computationally novel, societally relevant

**Option C — Duke BME 3D Scaffold Research**
- Context: Biomedical engineering research on tissue scaffold properties
- Lab-based experimental work with MATLAB analysis

Option B (hurricane ML, Zenodo publication) is probably the strongest Davidson application because the published paper demonstrates external validation and societal relevance.

## Requirements

- Application at davidsongifted.org
- Description of the significant work (the main component)
- Supporting documentation: paper, code, data, published work
- 3 recommendations (at least 1 expert in the field)
- Proof of publication or presentation (if applicable)
- Must be under 18 at time of application

## What a Strong Application Looks Like

- Work that goes beyond typical HS science fair — real novel contribution
- Expert validation (publication, presentation at conference, faculty collaboration)
- Work that could meaningfully impact a field
- Student demonstrably led the work, not just participated

## Your Competitive Profile

The main challenge: Davidson Fellows is extremely selective — typically 20-30 selected per year nationally. The work must be at a level that experts in the field would find genuinely novel. Consult your NC State mentor honestly about whether the hurricane model work is at this level.

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **November–December 2026:** Talk to NC State mentor — would they co-author a journal submission? Could the work be strengthened before February?
- **January 2027:** Application opens
- **February 2027:** Submit by deadline

## Notes

This is a "moon shot" application — very low acceptance rate, but the preparation process is valuable regardless. The recognition is extraordinary for college admissions.
