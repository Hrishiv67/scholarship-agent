# High School Internship Opportunities | Austin Economic Development | AustinTexas.gov

**Deadline:** 2026-02-28 (confirmed from official page quote)
**Deadline quote:** Applications will not be accepted after February 28, 2026.
**Opens:** Not stated on official page
**Award:** Six-week internship from June 1 – July 10, 2026. Interns work 20 hours per week. Interns are paid $13/hr. Multiple program tracks available: Emerging Leaders Summer Internship Program (ELSIP), Get Ready! Summer Internship Program (STEAM-based), Automotive/Entrepreneurial Internship Program, and Aviation Department Summer Internship Program.
**Eligibility:** Must be a resident of Austin, Texas and attend an Austin-area high school. Must be a rising sophomore, junior, senior or current senior. Must be between the ages of 15 and 18 years old at the time you apply. Must be available for the full duration of the program with no more than 3 days of absences.
