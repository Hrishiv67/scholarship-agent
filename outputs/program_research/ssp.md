# Summer Science Program (SSP)

**URL:** https://summerscience.org/  
**Application Opens:** ~October 2026 (estimated)  
**Deadline:** ~January 15, 2027 (estimated — check summerscience.org in October)  
**Award:** 6-week intensive residential program; ~$8,000 program fee; generous need-blind financial aid available (many students pay $0)  
**Tier:** Elite (~40 students per session, multiple sessions)  
**Eligibility:** HS students completing their junior year (rising seniors); strong math required  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

SSP is an intensive 6-week research program where students work in teams of 3 to complete a real scientific research project from scratch. Three tracks:
- **Astrophysics** (original track): determine orbit of an asteroid using telescope data + calculations
- **Biochemistry**: protein expression research
- **Genomics**: genomic analysis research

Locations: University of Colorado Boulder, New Mexico Tech, Westmont College (Astrophysics); Indiana University, UNC (Biochemistry); others.

**Note:** UNC Chapel Hill hosts an SSP Biochemistry session — this could make housing logistics easier.

---

## Requirements

- Application at summerscience.org
- Transcript
- SAT/ACT or SAT Math Subject Test scores (high scores needed, especially math)
- 2 teacher recommendations (math and science required)
- Short essays
- Personal statement about scientific interest
- Financial aid application (separate, need-blind aid available)

## Math Requirement

SSP is math-intensive. Astrophysics track requires comfort with calculus, vectors, and orbital mechanics. Biochemistry requires less math but still strong analytical skills. Check which track fits your background.

## What a Strong Application Looks Like

- Genuine fascination with the specific track (astrophysics, biochemistry, genomics)
- Evidence of independent scientific thinking — your research publications are very strong here
- Strong math grades and test scores
- Essays that show curiosity and depth, not just achievement
- Research experience is a significant advantage

## Your Competitive Profile

Published research (Zenodo) and NC State/Duke lab experience make you a strong applicant. The main variable is your comfort with the math requirements for the astrophysics track. If physics/calculus is strong, apply to astrophysics. If biology is stronger, biochemistry may be a better fit.

## Financial Aid

SSP financial aid is need-blind and generous — many students from families with $100k+ income pay less than half the listed cost, and lower-income students often pay $0. Apply for aid regardless of family income. Don't let the $8,000 sticker price deter you.

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **October 2026:** Application opens; identify which track is your best fit
- **October–November 2026:** Draft essays; ask math and science teachers for recommendations
- **December 2026:** Submit financial aid application
- **January 15, 2027:** Submit main application by deadline
- **March–April 2027:** Decisions released

## Notes

SSP alumni network is strong — many go on to top universities and research careers. The 6-week time commitment is significant but the program is considered one of the best HS experiences available in the US.
