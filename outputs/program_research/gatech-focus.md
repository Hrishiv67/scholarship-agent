# Georgia Tech FOCUS Fly-In

**URL:** https://admission.gatech.edu/first-year/campus-visit/focus
**Application Opens:** December to January (estimated)
**Deadline:** ~February 1 (estimated; verify at URL above)
**Award:** All-expenses campus visit to Atlanta, GA (ground transportation or flights, hotel, meals)
**Tier:** Competitive
**Eligibility:** Underrepresented high school juniors; Georgia Tech FOCUS typically includes underrepresented racial/ethnic minorities, first-generation college students, and students from low-income households interested in STEM
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

Georgia Tech's FOCUS (Finding Our Community Under the Stars, or similar; confirm current name at URL) is a diversity fly-in for high school juniors interested in STEM programs. Georgia Tech is one of the top public engineering universities in the country — its CS, aerospace, mechanical, and biomedical engineering programs are among the best nationally, and tuition for in-state students is among the lowest at any top-20 engineering school. As a North Carolina resident, Hrishiv would pay out-of-state tuition at Georgia Tech, but the school still tends to be meaningfully more affordable than private peers. Atlanta is a major city with a thriving tech and aerospace sector (Lockheed Martin, Delta, NCR, multiple Fortune 500 HQs). Georgia Tech's location, approximately 8 hours by car from Cary, NC, is closer than most elite engineering schools.

## Eligibility Note — South Asian Applicants

Georgia Tech FOCUS has historically targeted students from underrepresented racial/ethnic groups (Black, Hispanic/Latino, Native American, Pacific Islander), first-generation college students, and students from low-income backgrounds. South Asian students are generally **not** considered underrepresented at Georgia Tech, where the South Asian student population is already relatively large. **Hrishiv must confirm eligibility at the FOCUS URL before applying.** That said, Georgia Tech's FOCUS program has in past cycles used a somewhat broader definition — if first-generation or socioeconomic qualifiers are listed, that may create a path. This is also one of the fly-ins where a direct email to Georgia Tech admissions (admission@gatech.edu) is worth sending to ask.

## Requirements

Typical application components include:
- Short essay responses about STEM interests and personal background
- Unofficial transcript / GPA
- Extracurricular activities and honors
- Junior enrollment confirmation
- Parent/guardian contact information

## What to Highlight in Application

- **TARC 2nd nationally** — Georgia Tech's aerospace engineering program is one of the best in the world; national rocketry achievement is a direct and compelling hook
- **VEX Robotics top 150 worldwide** — Georgia Tech has a massive robotics presence (FIRST Robotics partnership, strong robotics research labs); this is directly relevant
- **Published ML research (NC State)** — Georgia Tech is one of the top ML research schools in the country (home of research groups that contributed foundational work in deep learning); ML research experience as a 10th grader stands out
- **Duke BME research** — Georgia Tech has a joint biomedical engineering department with Emory University; prior BME research signals fit
- **Books for Africa** — Georgia Tech has a strong service-learning culture; $85k raised demonstrates exceptional initiative
- Mention specific Georgia Tech programs or labs: the School of Aerospace Engineering, the Institute for Robotics and Intelligent Machines (IRIM), or the joint GT-Emory BME program

## Notes

Georgia Tech is arguably the strongest overall fit for Hrishiv's interests (aerospace + robotics + ML + BME) of any school on this list. It has all four of those programs at a high level in a single institution. The FOCUS fly-in, if eligible, is worth a significant application effort.
