# CMU EXPLORE Diversity Fly-In

**URL:** https://www.cmu.edu/admission/events/diversity-programs.html
**Application Opens:** September to early October (estimated)
**Deadline:** ~November 15 (estimated; verify at URL above)
**Award:** All-expenses campus visit to Pittsburgh, PA (flights, hotel, meals)
**Tier:** Highly Competitive
**Eligibility:** Underrepresented high school juniors; CMU typically includes underrepresented racial/ethnic minorities, first-generation college students, and students from low-income backgrounds
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

CMU EXPLORE is Carnegie Mellon University's diversity fly-in for high school juniors interested in exploring CMU's academic programs. CMU is consistently ranked as one of the top CS and engineering schools in the world — its School of Computer Science (SCS) is widely considered the best CS school globally, and its College of Engineering programs in robotics, ECE, and biomedical engineering are elite. EXPLORE runs for approximately two days and includes academic sessions specific to each school (SCS, Engineering, Science, etc.), student-led tours, faculty interactions, and financial aid workshops. Pittsburgh itself has undergone a significant tech transformation and now hosts major research and development operations for Google, Uber, and numerous AI/robotics startups.

## Eligibility Note — South Asian Applicants

CMU's diversity fly-in programs typically focus on students from underrepresented racial/ethnic groups and first-generation college students. South Asian students are generally **not** considered underrepresented under CMU's standard definitions, given higher representation rates in CS and engineering. **This is a critical eligibility check.** Hrishiv should read the EXPLORE eligibility page carefully and, if ambiguous, contact CMU's Office of Admission directly. CMU's CS and robotics programs attract many South Asian applicants, so the definition of "underrepresented" in this context may be applied more strictly than at a general-liberal-arts fly-in. However, if first-generation or socioeconomic criteria are listed independently, that may qualify.

## Requirements

Typical application components include:
- Short essay responses on academic interests and personal background (often 2–3 prompts, ~200–300 words each)
- Unofficial transcript
- Extracurricular activities and honors
- Interest in a specific CMU school or major
- Junior enrollment confirmation

## What to Highlight in Application

- **VEX Robotics top 150 worldwide** — CMU has the Robotics Institute, the premier robotics research center in the US; this is the single strongest alignment possible for this program
- **Published ML research (NC State)** — CMU's SCS is the home of machine learning research; publishable ML work as a 10th grader is exceptional and directly signals fit
- **TARC 2nd nationally** — aerospace-adjacent; relevant to CMU's mechanical and aerospace engineering programs
- **Duke BME research** — CMU has a growing BME department with strong industry ties
- **DECA ICDC** — CMU's Tepper School of Business is well-regarded; cross-disciplinary interest in business/STEM fits CMU's ethos
- Be specific: mention interest in CMU's SCS or Robotics track, specific labs (e.g., the Robotics Institute, the Human-Computer Interaction Institute), or faculty whose work you have read

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **September:** Verify eligibility at the URL; note the ~November 15 deadline (slightly later than most fly-ins)
- **October:** Begin drafts; use the extra time compared to October-deadline programs
- **Early November:** Finalize essays; have counselor or mentor review
- **By November 10:** Submit before the deadline

## Notes

CMU EXPLORE is unusual in having a mid-November deadline, which gives more prep time compared to October-deadline programs. This is one of the most important fly-ins to apply to if eligible, given the direct alignment between Hrishiv's robotics/ML/CS profile and CMU's strengths. Pittsburgh is a college town with a strong tech culture and surprisingly affordable living. CMU also has strong co-op and internship pipelines, particularly in robotics, AI, and autonomous systems. If EXPLORE eligibility does not apply, CMU's general admitted-student weekend (Playfair) is worth attending if admitted.
