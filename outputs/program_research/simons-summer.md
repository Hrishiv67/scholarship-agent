# Simons Summer Research Program

**URL:** https://www.stonybrook.edu/simons/
**Application Opens:** ~November 2026
**Deadline:** ~January 31, 2027
**Award:** $3,000 stipend; 7-week paid research experience at Stony Brook University, NY (late June – mid August)
**Tier:** Elite
**Eligibility:** Current HS juniors (rising seniors) at time of program; must be enrolled in a US HS; Long Island NY residence NOT required; highly competitive nationally
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

## Overview

The Simons Summer Research Program at Stony Brook University places approximately 30–40 HS students per summer in faculty-mentored research labs across natural sciences, social sciences, and engineering. Students work directly in a faculty lab for 7 weeks, attending weekly seminars and culminating in a research symposium. The $3,000 stipend covers living expenses; Stony Brook provides on-campus housing. This is one of the most prestigious paid HS research programs in the country — alumni have gone on to Intel/Regeneron STS finalists and top-university admissions.

## Requirements

- Application through the Stony Brook portal
- Transcript (strong academic record expected; competitive GPAs 3.8+)
- Two teacher/mentor letters of recommendation
- Personal essay describing research interests and prior experience
- Resume or activity list
- Applicants may indicate preferred research areas (biology, physics, CS, engineering, environmental science)
