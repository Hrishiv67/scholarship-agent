# University of Michigan Engineering Fly-In (EngX)

**URL:** https://admissions.umich.edu/discover-explore/diversity-programs
**Application Opens:** Early September (estimated)
**Deadline:** ~October 15 (estimated; verify at URL above)
**Award:** All-expenses campus visit to Ann Arbor, MI (flights, hotel, meals)
**Tier:** Competitive
**Eligibility:** Underrepresented high school juniors interested in engineering; Michigan typically includes underrepresented racial/ethnic minorities, first-generation college students, and students from low-income backgrounds
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

Michigan's EngX (Engineering Experience) diversity fly-in is hosted by the College of Engineering at the University of Michigan, one of the top public engineering schools in the country. The program runs for two to three days and includes lab tours, faculty panels, design challenges, residential life experiences, and financial aid sessions. U-M Engineering has strong programs in aerospace, robotics, CS, and biomedical engineering — all directly aligned with Hrishiv's interests. Michigan's robotics program in particular is nationally ranked and has ties to serious research and industry partnerships.

## Eligibility Note — South Asian Applicants

Michigan's diversity programs for engineering have historically included students from underrepresented racial/ethnic groups (Black, Hispanic/Latino, Native American, Pacific Islander), first-generation college students, and students from low-income backgrounds. South Asian students are generally **not** categorized as underrepresented at most engineering schools given higher representation rates. **Hrishiv must check the specific eligibility criteria on the program page** before applying. If Michigan's definition extends to first-generation or socioeconomic factors, there may be a qualifying path. If the program is narrowly restricted by race/ethnicity and excludes South Asian, it is not worth applying.

## Requirements

Typical application components include:
- Short essays or responses about interest in engineering and personal background
- Unofficial transcript
- List of activities and honors
- High school junior enrollment confirmation
- Parent/guardian information

## What to Highlight in Application

- **TARC 2nd nationally** — aerospace and rocketry is directly tied to Michigan's aerospace engineering program, one of the strongest in the country
- **VEX Robotics top 150 worldwide** — Michigan has one of the nation's best robotics programs; this is a strong hook
- **NC State hurricane ML research (published on Zenodo)** — shows readiness for research-level coursework
- **Duke BME research** — relevant to U-M's biomedical engineering department
- Mention specific Michigan labs or faculty if you have done any research on them (e.g., the Robotics Institute, EECS)
- DECA ICDC and Books for Africa add leadership depth outside pure STEM
