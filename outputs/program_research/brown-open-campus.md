# Brown Open Campus Days

**URL:** https://admission.brown.edu/connect/campus-visit-program
**Application Opens:** Early to mid-October (estimated)
**Deadline:** ~November 1 (estimated; verify at URL above)
**Award:** All-expenses campus visit to Providence, RI (flights, hotel, meals)
**Tier:** Competitive
**Eligibility:** Underrepresented high school juniors; Brown typically includes underrepresented racial/ethnic minorities, first-generation college students, and students from low-income households
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

Brown Open Campus Days is Brown University's diversity fly-in program for high school juniors. The two-to-three day program is among the more genuinely exploratory fly-ins — Brown's Open Curriculum (students design their own course of study with no required core) makes campus visits particularly important, as prospective students need to understand the culture to know if it fits. Activities typically include class visits, student panels, lab tours, overnight homestays with current students, and financial aid presentations. Brown's STEM programs are strong: Applied Mathematics, CS, Biomedical Engineering, and Cognitive Neuroscience are popular and well-resourced. The School of Engineering is smaller and more collaborative than peer schools.

## Eligibility Note — South Asian Applicants

Brown's diversity programs have traditionally targeted students from groups underrepresented in higher education, primarily Black, Hispanic/Latino, Native American, and Pacific Islander students, as well as first-generation college students. South Asian students are generally **not** classified as underrepresented under standard institutional definitions. **Hrishiv should verify Brown's specific eligibility criteria before investing time in the application.** The admissions page may clarify whether first-generation status or socioeconomic background independently qualifies. Brown's admissions office (admission@brown.edu) is generally responsive to direct questions about eligibility.

## Requirements

Typical application components include:
- Short essay responses (Brown often uses prompts related to intellectual curiosity or community)
- Unofficial transcript
- Activities list
- Confirmation of junior enrollment
- Parent/guardian contact and educational background

## What to Highlight in Application

- **Brown's Open Curriculum connection:** Frame interest in exploring across aerospace, CS, and BME without being locked into a rigid track — this maps perfectly onto Brown's curriculum philosophy
- **Published ML research (NC State)** — intellectual initiative that aligns with Brown's research culture
- **Duke BME research** — Brown has a strong BIOL/BME nexus; this is directly relevant
- **VEX Robotics and TARC** — shows hands-on technical depth
- **Books for Africa** — Brown students care deeply about social impact; this leadership story resonates strongly here
- Science Olympiad shows breadth of scientific curiosity, which fits Brown's interdisciplinary culture

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **Early October:** Check URL for application opening; verify eligibility
- **Mid-October:** Draft responses — Brown prompts often ask about your intellectual interests or something you've explored outside the classroom
- **Late October:** Finalize and review with counselor
- **By October 28:** Submit before November 1

## Notes

Brown's Open Curriculum is genuinely unusual and worth understanding before visiting — students can take any courses they want, concentrations are lighter than majors elsewhere, and there are no distribution requirements. This culture attracts intellectually curious, self-directed students, which describes Hrishiv's profile well. Providence, RI is a compact city with a strong food and arts scene. Brown is also part of the Ivy League and meets 100% of demonstrated financial need. Even if the fly-in does not work out, Brown's general visit days (offered through the fall) are free to attend on your own.
