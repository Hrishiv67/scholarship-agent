## Overview

The National Institute of Standards and Technology Summer High School Internship Program (SHIP) places HS students in NIST research labs in Gaithersburg, MD (main campus) and Boulder, CO. NIST's mission covers measurement science, standards, and technology across physics, chemistry, materials science, computer science, and engineering. Students are matched to a specific NIST researcher/mentor and work on a defined project for 8 weeks. At the end, students present a poster at NIST's summer student symposium. NIST is roughly 1.5 hours from Cary (Gaithersburg MD) — housing is not provided but some students commute or arrange temporary housing.

## Requirements

- Online application at NIST careers portal

- One or two letters of recommendation from science/math teachers

- US citizenship required (NIST is a federal agency)
