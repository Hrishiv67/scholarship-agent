# National Merit Scholarship

**URL:** https://www.nationalmerit.org/  
**Application:** NO separate application — entry is through the PSAT/NMSQT  
**PSAT Date:** October 2026 (register at Green Hope HS — ask your counselor)  
**Notification Timeline:** Semifinalists notified September 2027; Finalists January 2028; Scholars spring 2028  
**Award:** $2,500 National Merit Scholarship; plus corporate and college-sponsored scholarships (many up to $10,000/year)  
**Tier:** Competitive  
**Eligibility:** HS juniors (Class of 2028) who take the PSAT/NMSQT in October 2026; US citizen  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

## ACTION REQUIRED

Register for the PSAT at Green Hope HS for October 2026. This is your ONE chance — if you miss it, you cannot enter the National Merit competition for your class year. Ask your school counselor in September.

## How It Works

1. **October 2026:** Take PSAT/NMSQT at Green Hope HS
2. **December 2026:** Scores released — check your Selection Index score
3. **September 2027:** Top ~16,000 students nationally named Semifinalists (letter from school)
4. **October 2027:** Semifinalists complete application: GPA, activities, essay, SAT score confirmation
5. **January 2028:** ~15,000 named Finalists
6. **Spring 2028:** ~7,500 winners selected from Finalists

## Qualifying Score (Selection Index)

North Carolina's typical cutoff is ~218-222 (out of 228) Selection Index — one of the higher state cutoffs. Selection Index = 2×(Reading+Writing score) + Math score, on the PSAT scale.

Your SAT 1430 is a strong indicator — if that translates well to PSAT, you're in competitive range. The PSAT score matters most; SAT is used only to confirm the PSAT later.

## The Application (if you become a Semifinalist)

- Academic record (transcript)
- Extracurricular activities list
- One essay (typically: describe a meaningful activity or leadership role)
- Confirmation SAT score (must match PSAT performance)
- Recommendation from school principal

## Corporate and College Scholarships

If named a Finalist, you become eligible for hundreds of corporate-sponsored National Merit scholarships (many are $500–$10,000/year) — IBM, Coca-Cola, and many others have these. College-sponsored National Merit scholarships at some schools are $20,000+/year.

## Prep

- Take an NMSQT practice test (College Board's "Official PSAT Practice" on Khan Academy is free and excellent)
- Focus on reading comprehension and grammar — the PSAT weighs these heavily
- October 2026: Take the real PSAT

## Notes

This is a one-time opportunity tied to 11th grade. Missing the PSAT is not recoverable. The actual scholarship odds are good for strong students — the main challenge is the qualifying score cutoff.
