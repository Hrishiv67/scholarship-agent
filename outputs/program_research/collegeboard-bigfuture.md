# College Board BigFuture Scholarships

**URL:** https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/pay-for-college/bigfuture-scholarships-2027
**Application Opens:** ~September 2026 (new cycle typically opens in fall)
**Deadline:** Monthly entry windows; no single hard deadline
**Award:** Monthly drawings: $500–$40,000 per month in total prizes; individual awards typically $500–$2,000
**Tier:** Accessible
**Eligibility:** HS students with a College Board (SAT) account; US resident; complete specific profile tasks to earn entries; no essay required
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

## Overview

College Board's BigFuture Scholarships are a sweepstakes-style scholarship program tied to the BigFuture college planning platform. Students earn entries into monthly drawings by completing profile tasks: adding colleges to their list, completing the CSS Profile walkthrough, attending virtual college fairs, and similar low-effort activities. Each completed task generates one or more entries into monthly drawings. Drawings are held monthly throughout the academic year (typically October through June). There is no essay, no competition, and no merit review — it is pure chance weighted by number of entries earned.

## Requirements

- Free College Board / BigFuture account (same login as SAT registration)
- Complete the available profile tasks to earn drawing entries
- US high school student
- No essay, no recommendations, no GPA threshold
