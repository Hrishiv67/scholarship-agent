## Overview

The American Physical Society runs several scholarship programs, most notably: (1) the **APS Scholarships for Minority Undergraduate Physics Majors** (specifically for underrepresented minorities — check eligibility), (2) the **APS Physics Olympiad Scholarships** (tied to competition participation), and (3) general APS foundation scholarships for physics students. APS also partners with industry to fund specific scholarships. The HS-to-college eligibility bridge exists for some programs. Rocketry and applied physics background is relevant, though APS primarily looks for physics majors heading into academic physics careers.

## Requirements

- Application through aps.org
- Transcript with strong math and science record (AP Physics C is relevant)
- Essay on physics interest and career goals
- Letters of recommendation from physics teachers or research mentors
- For some programs: demonstrated minority status (verify which apply)
- Commitment to pursuing physics or closely related field in college
