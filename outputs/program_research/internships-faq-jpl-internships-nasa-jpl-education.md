## Notes
High school internships (JPL SpaceSHIP) are not regularly funded. Page states: 'Each year, the Academic Engagement Office determines if it will be possible to offer high school internships for the subsequent summer.' Student should check program page December/January for summer 2027 opportunities. Most Academic Engagement Office internships require minimum 10-week commitment. General recommendation for summer internships is to apply October–February, but no specific 2027 dates confirmed on this page.
