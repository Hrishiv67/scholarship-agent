# Clark Scholars Program

**URL:** https://www.clarkscholars.ttu.edu/  
**Application Opens:** ~November 2026 (estimated)  
**Deadline:** ~February 6, 2027 (first Friday of February — consistent historically)  
**Award:** 7-week paid research at Texas Tech; $750/week stipend (~$5,250 total); free housing + meals  
**Tier:** Elite (12 scholars selected nationally from ~1,000 applicants)  
**Eligibility:** HS juniors and seniors; US citizen or permanent resident; 17+ by program start  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

12 students selected nationally — ~1.2% acceptance. Each student matched with a TTU faculty mentor for 7 weeks of original research. Real stipend ($750/week) is among the highest for any HS program. Program runs June–July.

---

## Requirements

- Online application at clarkscholars.ttu.edu
- Official transcripts
- SAT/ACT scores (competitive range expected)
- 2-3 teacher/mentor recommendations
- Short essays
- Research interests statement

## What a Strong Application Looks Like

- Prior independent research — Zenodo publication is directly relevant
- Strong academic record (3.9+ GPA, high test scores)
- Specific research interests aligned with Texas Tech faculty
- Recommendations from research mentors, not just classroom teachers
- Initiative: self-directed projects, research beyond school

## Your Competitive Profile

**Strengths:** Published Zenodo paper (very rare for HS); NC State + Duke mentor relationships; ML in atmospheric science fits TTU atmospheric science department; TARC 2nd nationally.

**Action item:** Browse Texas Tech faculty pages before applying — mention specific labs you'd want to join. Specificity = stronger application.

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **October 2026:** Browse TTU faculty pages; identify 3-4 labs of interest
- **November 2026:** Application opens; start essays immediately
- **December 2026 – January 2027:** Finalize essays + request recommendations
- **February 6, 2027:** Submit deadline

## Notes
