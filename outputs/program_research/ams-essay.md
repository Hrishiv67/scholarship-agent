## Overview

AMS (American Meteorological Society) is the leading professional organization for atmospheric science. Their scholarships support students entering weather, climate, and related fields. The essay is the main differentiator.

## Requirements

- Online application at ametsoc.org
- Official transcript
- Short essay about interest in atmospheric/oceanic science and career goals
- One recommendation letter
- Statement of planned college major (atmospheric science or related field)

## Essay Approach

Your essay writes itself:
1. The problem: hurricane track prediction affects millions of people's lives and emergency response
2. Your work: ML model predicting hurricane tracks from historical data (NC State collaboration)
3. What you learned: specific insight about model accuracy, data quality, or a surprising finding
4. Your goal: pursue atmospheric science + ML in college and beyond

Approximately 500-750 words. Be specific — name the model, the dataset, the accuracy metrics.

## Your Competitive Profile

Most AMS scholarship applicants are interested in meteorology from watching the Weather Channel. You have published research in hurricane modeling. This is a significant differentiator in a relatively accessible scholarship pool.

## Notes

AMS offers several different scholarships at once — check all of them when the application opens, as some have different requirements (financial need, women-only, etc.). Apply to all for which you're eligible. The "Father James B. Macelwane Annual Award in Meteorology" specifically rewards HS student research in atmospheric science — high relevance.
