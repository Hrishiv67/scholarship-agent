# Wake County Public Schools Foundation Scholarships

**URL:** https://www.wcpss.net/Page/2147

**Deadline:** ~February 2027 (varies by individual scholarship)
**Award:** $500–$5,000 per scholarship; some renewable
**Tier:** Accessible
**Eligibility:** Students enrolled in Wake County Public Schools (Green Hope HS qualifies); generally for graduating seniors (Class of 2028 applies spring 2028, but check for junior-eligible programs); NC resident
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

## Overview

The Wake County Public Schools Foundation administers a portfolio of scholarships funded by local businesses, alumni, and community donors. Scholarships are coordinated through the WCPSS scholarship portal and Green Hope HS's guidance department. Some scholarships are school-specific (Green Hope alumni or community donors tied to the school), while others are district-wide. Award amounts are modest ($500–$5,000) but competition is local/district-level, meaning lower competition than national programs. These awards are typically applied toward college tuition.

## Requirements

- WCPSS student enrollment (Green Hope HS qualifies)
- Transcript
- Essay(s) — topics vary by scholarship (community service, career goals, overcoming adversity)
- Letters of recommendation (1–2 typically)
- Some scholarships require financial need documentation
- Most require graduating senior status — confirm eligibility timing

The guidance counselor is the most important resource here — they know which Green Hope-specific scholarships exist beyond the main WCPSS portal. Some local Cary businesses also fund Green Hope-specific scholarships that are distributed through the school rather than the foundation. Ask explicitly: "Are there any scholarships specific to Green Hope HS students?" Note that most of these scholarships are for graduating seniors, so the primary application window for Class of 2028 is spring 2028 — but preparing materials now avoids a rush during college application season.
