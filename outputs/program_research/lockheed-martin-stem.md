# Lockheed Martin STEM Scholarship

**URL:** https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/who-we-are/communities/stem-education/lm-scholarship-program.html  
**Application Opens:** ~October 2026 (estimated)  
**Deadline:** ~February 15, 2027 (estimated — check lockheedmartin.com in October)  
**Award:** $10,000/year scholarship (4 years = $40,000 total)  
**Tier:** Competitive  
**Eligibility:** HS seniors pursuing aerospace, computer science, or engineering; 3.5+ GPA; US citizen  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

Lockheed Martin is the world's largest defense contractor — aerospace, missiles, aircraft, and space systems. They specifically fund students heading into aerospace engineering, computer science, and related STEM fields. This scholarship directly aligns with your profile.

---

## Requirements

- Online application at lockheedmartin.com
- Transcript (3.5+ GPA required)
- US citizenship
- Short essays
- Intended college major in aerospace, engineering, or CS
- Teacher/counselor recommendation

## Essay Approach

Your narrative:
1. TARC rocketry 2nd nationally — direct aerospace/propulsion experience
2. AI altitude prediction model — ML in aerospace engineering context
3. AP Physics + aerodynamics background
4. Interested in aerospace engineering + AI applications (autonomous vehicles, guidance systems)

Connect to Lockheed Martin's actual work: F-35 fighter jet, Orion spacecraft, missile defense systems. Show you know what the company does and why you want to contribute.

## Your Competitive Profile

TARC national 2nd place is directly relevant to a company that builds rockets and missiles. This is one of the cleanest profile-to-scholarship matches on your list. The main requirement is 3.5+ GPA — verify your GPA exceeds this before applying.

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **October 2026:** Check lockheedmartin.com for application opening
- **November–December 2026:** Draft essays
- **January 2027:** Request recommendation from AP Physics teacher or TARC mentor
- **February 2027:** Submit by deadline

## Notes

Lockheed Martin also offers internships — being a Lockheed Martin scholar creates a pipeline to internships at their facilities (Palmdale CA, Orlando FL, Fort Worth TX, RTP area). Worth mentioning internship interest in the application.
