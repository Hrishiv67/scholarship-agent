## Notes
Program runs March 30, 2026 to April 16, 2027 with three stages: Stage 1 (Early 2026 - hybrid discovery), Stage 2 (Spring 2026-Spring 2027 - paid earn-and-learn), Stage 3 (Spring 2027 - credentials/apprenticeship transition). Application submitted to Career.Pathways@Hired.org or faxed to 612-338-1375; applicant will be contacted by employment counselor within 5 days. Contact: Chan Xiong, 651-301-2586, chan.xiong@hired.org. Program is free for eligible participants (DEED Drive for Five grant).
