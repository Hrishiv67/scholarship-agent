## Notes
Program runs March 30, 2026 – April 16, 2027 in three stages: Stage 1 (Discovery, Early 2026, hybrid); Stage 2 (Earn & Learn Pre-Apprenticeship, Spring 2026 – Spring 2027, in-person); Stage 3 (Credentials, Spring 2027). Student is rising junior (11th grade) so will not have high school diploma yet; would need to complete diploma or obtain GED before participating. Program is free for eligible participants (DEED Drive for Five grant). Application via Career Pathways program application, emailed to Career.Pathways@Hired.org or faxed to 612-338-1375.
