# Prudential Spirit of Community Award

**URL:** https://spirit.prudential.com/  
**Application Opens:** ~September 2026 (estimated)  
**Deadline:** ~November 1, 2026 (estimated — check spirit.prudential.com in September)  
**Award:** State Honorees: $1,000 each (2 per state); National Honorees: $5,000 each (10 total) + Washington DC trip  
**Tier:** Accessible  
**Eligibility:** HS students grades 5-12; US residents; based on community service impact  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

The Prudential Spirit of Community Award recognizes students for outstanding community service. The key is a single, focused community service project with documented impact — not a list of activities.

---

## Your Best Application: Books for Africa

This scholarship is essentially made for your Books for Africa project:
- **What you did:** Organized an international book drive shipping $85,000+ worth of books to Kenya and Tanzania
- **Scale:** International scope at age ~15-16 is extraordinary
- **Documentation:** Number of books collected, dollar value, weight shipped, countries and communities served, number of students reached
- **Innovation:** How did you organize it? What obstacles did you overcome?

This is a standout application. Most applicants organize local food drives or charity events. An international humanitarian project with $85,000 impact is top-tier.

## Requirements

- Application at spirit.prudential.com
- Description of community service project (main essay)
- Documentation of impact (how you measured results)
- Teacher/principal endorsement
- No test scores or transcript required

## Essay Approach

Tell the Books for Africa story:
1. **The problem:** Educational resource inequity in sub-Saharan Africa
2. **Your initiative:** How you organized the drive, recruited community support
3. **The impact:** Specific numbers — books, dollar value, students served
4. **What you learned:** Logistics of international giving, community organizing, leadership

Keep it focused on one project — don't spread across multiple activities.

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **September 2026:** Check spirit.prudential.com for current application
- **October 2026:** Draft essay with Books for Africa documentation; teacher endorsement
- **November 2026:** Submit by deadline

## Notes

State honorees are selected first, then national. Being named a State Honoree alone is worth noting on college applications. The $5,000 national award + DC trip is the top prize. NC is a large state with many applicants — quantify your impact specifically to stand out.
