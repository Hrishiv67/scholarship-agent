**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

## Overview

COSMOS is a California state-funded 4-week residential STEM program hosted across four UC campuses: UC Davis, UC San Diego, UC Irvine, and UC Santa Cruz. Each campus offers several "clusters" — intensive mini-courses led by UC faculty in specific STEM areas. Relevant clusters across campuses include: robotics and autonomous systems (UC Davis, UC San Diego), machine learning and data science (UC Irvine), biomedical engineering (UC San Diego), and astrophysics. Students take one cluster deeply rather than broad survey content. Each cluster results in a research project or engineering prototype.

## Requirements

- Application through the COSMOS central portal at cosmos-ucop.ucdavis.edu
- Transcript
- Two teacher letters of recommendation
- Personal statement describing STEM interest and reason for choosing specific cluster
- Math and science coursework list
- Financial aid application available separately (fee waiver or partial scholarship)
