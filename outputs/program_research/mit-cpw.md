## Overview

MIT Campus Preview Weekend is a four-day event held each spring (typically April) for students who have been admitted to MIT's incoming first-year class. It is not a diversity fly-in program in the traditional sense — there is no separate application, no essay to write, and no selection process. If MIT admits you, you are invited to CPW. MIT covers travel, lodging, and meals for admitted students to attend.

CPW is one of the most celebrated admitted-student weekends in higher education. Activities include hack nights, lab tours, department open houses, dorm explorations, faculty panels, and a culture of MIT-flavored events organized by current students. It is widely regarded as the most accurate window into MIT student culture available to prospective students.

## Why This File Exists

This entry is included to clarify that CPW cannot and should not be treated as a proactive application opportunity in the way the other 13 programs on this list are. **Do not plan logistics around CPW unless MIT admission has occurred.** Energy that might go into "preparing" for CPW is better spent on:

1. Strengthening the MIT application itself (essays, activities)
2. Applying to the standalone fly-ins on this list that do have open applications

## What MIT Looks For (Application Context)

Since CPW is downstream of MIT admission, the relevant question is: what does MIT look for in applicants? MIT admissions is famously holistic and focuses heavily on intellectual curiosity, demonstrated passion for math and science, and collaborative problem-solving. Specific signals that are strong for Hrishiv's MIT application include:

- **Published ML research (NC State / Zenodo)** — original research published before junior year is rare and meaningful to MIT admissions
- **TARC 2nd nationally** — MIT's aeronautics tradition runs deep; national rocketry is a direct cultural fit
- **VEX Robotics top 150 worldwide** — hands-on robotics achievement is highly valued
- **Duke BME research** — demonstrates initiative beyond the classroom
- **Science Olympiad** — common among admitted MIT students; shows broad scientific curiosity
- **Books for Africa** — MIT students are expected to care about impact; this demonstrates it concretely

## MIT Visit Before Admission

MIT offers standard campus tours and information sessions year-round through the Admissions Office (admissions.mit.edu/visit). These are open to any prospective student and are worth scheduling for a trip to Boston/Cambridge. MIT's campus in Cambridge is walkable and accessible from Logan Airport. A self-funded visit to sit in on open lectures (MIT OpenCourseWare classes are sometimes auditable in person) can strengthen the sense of fit before applying.

## Notes

MIT's overall admission rate is under 4%. The application is due in late October (QuestBridge) or November 1 (EA) / January 1 (Regular Decision). MIT does not offer merit scholarships — aid is entirely need-based, and MIT meets 100% of demonstrated need with grants. For families with income under $140,000, MIT typically charges $0 in tuition. This makes MIT genuinely affordable for a wide range of families, and the financial aid session at CPW — if admitted — is highly informative.
