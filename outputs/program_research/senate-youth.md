# US Senate Youth Program

**URL:** https://www.ussenateyouth.org/  
**Application Opens:** ~September 2026 (estimated)  
**Deadline:** ~October 1, 2026 (very early — check immediately in September)  
**Award:** $10,000 college scholarship + week-long Washington DC program meeting US Senators and government leaders  
**Tier:** Elite (2 students selected per state)  
**Eligibility:** HS juniors or seniors who hold an **elected** student government or civic office; nominated via NC DPI  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Critical Eligibility Requirement

**You must currently hold an ELECTED student government or civic office to apply.** This is not about extracurricular activities — it must be an elected position. Examples that qualify:
- Student body president, vice president, or secretary (elected by student vote)
- Class president
- Elected student government representative or delegate
- Elected officer in a recognized civic organization

**Check whether any of your current roles are elected positions.** If you hold a DECA elected officer position, VEX Robotics team leadership elected by the team, or any school government role, that may qualify.

## Nomination Process

The Senate Youth Program does not accept direct applications. Process in NC:
1. Check if you hold a qualifying elected office
2. Apply through NC DPI (NC Department of Public Instruction) — they administer the state competition
3. School principal must certify your elected office
4. NC DPI selects 2 NC delegates from all applicants

Contact your Green Hope HS guidance counselor about the NC nomination process.

## What a Strong Application Looks Like

- Genuine civic leadership through an elected office
- Articulate essays about government, democracy, and public service
- Strong academic record
- Community service and leadership

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **September 2026:** Check if you hold a qualifying elected office; contact guidance counselor about NC nomination
- **Early October 2026:** Submit materials by NC deadline (likely before October 1 national deadline)

## Notes

If you don't currently hold an elected office, you cannot apply — this is a hard requirement. If you do, this is a high-prestige, $10,000 award worth prioritizing immediately given the early October deadline. The DC program includes meetings with sitting US Senators and Executive Branch officials.
