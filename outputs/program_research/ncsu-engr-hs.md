## Overview

NC State's College of Engineering runs multiple HS programs under one umbrella. Key programs include: (1) **Engineering Summer Intensive (ESI)** — week-long residential for rising 10th–12th graders introducing engineering disciplines; (2) **HS Internship Program** — research placements in NC State faculty labs for academically strong rising juniors/seniors; (3) **NCSU Research Experiences** — informal research partnerships for motivated students with prior NC State connections; (4) **NCSU GRIP (Graduate Research Internship Program)** — technically for grad students but sometimes has HS research tracks.
