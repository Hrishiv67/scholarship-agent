# NC State GRIP High School Research Program

**URL:** https://grip.ncsu.edu/high-school/  
**Application Opens:** ~January 2027 (estimated)  
**Deadline:** ~February 1, 2027 (estimated — check grip.ncsu.edu in January)  
**Award:** Paid 8-week summer research stipend; work with NC State faculty mentor  
**Tier:** Competitive  
**Eligibility:** HS students; NC residents preferred; strong STEM background required  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

GRIP (Graduates Research in Progress) includes a HS track where students work with NC State faculty on real research for 8 weeks in summer. Given your existing NC State hurricane ML collaboration, you likely already know the lab and process.

---

## Competitive Advantage

You already have a working relationship with NC State faculty from your hurricane tracking research. A direct application to continue that research — or an adjacent project — with an NC State mentor is exceptionally strong. Most applicants are cold-applying without prior relationships.

## Requirements

- Application at grip.ncsu.edu/high-school/
- Transcript
- Short personal statement
- Teacher recommendation
- Description of research interests
- Potentially: faculty mentor willing to host you (prior relationship helps enormously)

## What a Strong Application Looks Like

- Prior research experience in the relevant lab (you have this)
- Clear research proposal or interest statement tied to NC State's actual research areas
- Strong academic record
- Recommendation from current NC State mentor (if applicable) is the strongest possible letter

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **November–December 2026:** Email your NC State hurricane research mentor about returning for summer 2027 — ask if they're open to hosting you through GRIP or independently
- **January 2027:** Application opens; apply with your mentor contact included
- **February 2027:** Submit before deadline

## Notes

If your NC State mentor is willing to host you directly (outside GRIP), that's even better — ask both. Prior student researchers at a lab typically get priority consideration. This is your highest-probability local research program given the existing relationship.
