**Deadline quote:** The deadline to apply is Wednesday, September 30, 2026, at 5 pm Eastern.
**Opens:** Not stated on official page
**Award:** $20,000 college scholarship. 150 Coca-Cola Scholars selected annually. Phase 1 (online application, no essays/transcript/recommendations required) → Phase 2 (essays, transcript, recommendation; ~1% become Semifinalists) → Phase 3 (Regional Finalists interview late January/early February; ~250 advance) → Phase 4 (150 selected, required Coke Scholars Weekend in Atlanta in April).
**Eligibility:** High school seniors (Class of 2027) graduating during 2026-2027 school year, currently enrolled, attending school in 50 US states, DC, Puerto Rico, or select DoD schools. Must be US Citizen, US National, US Permanent Resident, Refugee, Asylee, Cuban-Haitian Entrant, or Humanitarian Parolee. Minimum 3.0 GPA. Planning to pursue degree at accredited US post-secondary institution. Cannot be children/grandchildren of Coca-Cola Company employees or retirees receiving benefits.
