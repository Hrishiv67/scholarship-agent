## Overview

One of the most prestigious HS scholarships in the US. 150 Scholars selected from ~100,000 applicants. The foundation looks for student leaders who are making a difference in their communities — not just academic achievers.

---

## Requirements

- Online application (no paper materials)
- Short-answer essays
- Extracurricular activities list
- No teacher recommendations required in Phase 1
- Phase 2 (semifinalists): additional essays + recommendations
- Phase 3 (finalists): in-person interview in Atlanta

## Typical Essay Questions (vary slightly by year)

Phase 1 short answers (200-500 words each, based on prior years):
1. Describe your most meaningful activity and why it matters to you
2. What community impact have you had and how do you measure it?
3. Describe a challenge and what you learned from it
4. What are your goals and how have your experiences prepared you?

Check coca-colascholarsfoundation.org in August for the exact 2027 prompts.

## What a Strong Application Looks Like

- **Community impact over breadth:** One or two activities with deep, measurable impact beats 10 surface-level activities
- Best scholarship essay narratives: Books for Africa (organized international book drive, $85,000 worth shipped to Kenya/Tanzania) and Robotics for All (teaching younger students)
- Evidence of leadership in organizations, not just participation
- Passion and vision — where are you going? What problem will you solve?
- Specific numbers: books donated, students taught, funds raised, competitions won
