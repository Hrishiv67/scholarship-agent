# Vanderbilt YES! Fly-In

**URL:** https://admissions.vanderbilt.edu/discover/diversity-programs/
**Application Opens:** Early September (estimated)
**Deadline:** ~October 15 (estimated; verify at URL above)
**Award:** All-expenses campus visit to Nashville, TN (flights, hotel, meals)
**Tier:** Competitive
**Eligibility:** Underrepresented high school juniors, typically defined as Black, Hispanic/Latino, Native American, first-generation college students, and students from low-income backgrounds
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

Vanderbilt YES! (Your Exceptional Story) is Vanderbilt University's signature diversity fly-in program for high school juniors. The multi-day visit includes academic exploration sessions, financial aid workshops, panels with current students, and campus tours. Nashville is a mid-sized city with a strong culture scene, and Vanderbilt's Peabody College (education), School of Engineering, and strong pre-med pipeline are all relevant for STEM students. Vanderbilt is also known for generous merit and need-based aid, making the financial aid session particularly valuable.

## Eligibility Note — South Asian Applicants

Vanderbilt YES! has historically focused on students from groups that are underrepresented in higher education. The program commonly lists Black, Hispanic/Latino, Native American, and Pacific Islander students as the primary target groups, with first-generation and low-income students sometimes included as well. South Asian students are **generally not** considered underrepresented under most institutional definitions, and Hrishiv should **verify eligibility on the program page before applying**. If first-generation college student status or financial need is a qualifying criterion, that could open the door. If the program is race/ethnicity-restricted to groups that do not include South Asian, do not apply — it is not worth the time investment.

## Requirements

Typical application components include:
- Short personal statement or responses to short-answer prompts
- Unofficial transcript / GPA
- List of extracurricular activities
- Current junior enrollment confirmation
- Parent/guardian contact information

## What to Highlight in Application

- **TARC national runner-up** — relevant to Vanderbilt's engineering programs; shows sustained technical excellence
- **VEX Robotics top 150 worldwide** — distinguishes the application from typical STEM candidates
- **Published ML research at NC State** — rare for a 10th grader; emphasizes intellectual ambition
- **DECA ICDC qualifier** — signals business/leadership dimension alongside STEM
- **Books for Africa ($85k raised)** — demonstrates exceptional community impact and initiative
- Frame interest in Vanderbilt's specific engineering or CS offerings; the School of Engineering is smaller and more tight-knit than peers

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **August:** Check URL for application opening date
- **Early September:** Draft short-answer responses as soon as the portal opens
- **Late September:** Refine essays; have a teacher or counselor review
- **By October 10:** Submit ahead of the deadline

## Notes

Vanderbilt is known for rolling review of fly-in applications, so early submission can improve chances. The YES! program is competitive — typically a few dozen spots. Nashville's proximity to NC (a 7-hour drive or short flight) is a logistical plus. Even if this particular fly-in does not work out, Vanderbilt offers other campus visit programs worth exploring. Always confirm current eligibility criteria and deadline on the official URL before applying.
