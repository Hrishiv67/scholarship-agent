# Harvey Mudd College FAST Fly-In

**URL:** https://www.hmc.edu/admission/fast/
**Application Opens:** October to early November (estimated)
**Deadline:** ~December 1 (estimated; verify at URL above — this deadline is earlier than it appears given its December date, as it falls during a busy application season)
**Award:** All-expenses campus visit to Claremont, CA (flights, hotel, meals)
**Tier:** Competitive
**Eligibility:** Underrepresented high school juniors; HMC historically includes underrepresented racial/ethnic groups, women in STEM, first-generation college students, and students from low-income backgrounds
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

Harvey Mudd's FAST (Fly-in for Aspiring Scientists and Technologists) fly-in brings underrepresented high school juniors to the Claremont Colleges campus for a multi-day immersive visit. Harvey Mudd is a highly specialized STEM-focused liberal arts college — it is small (~900 students), all STEM, and consistently produces the highest median mid-career salaries of any undergraduate institution in the United States. All HMC students complete a common core of math, science, and engineering before specializing, and every senior participates in an industry-partnered clinic project. Majors include CS, Engineering (a flexible integrated major), Math, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology. The Claremont Consortium allows students to take courses at Pomona, Scripps, Claremont McKenna, and Pitzer — adding humanities, social sciences, and language courses.

## Eligibility Note — South Asian Applicants

Harvey Mudd's FAST program is notable for explicitly including **women in STEM** as a qualifying group, and some years has used a broader definition of underrepresented that goes beyond race/ethnicity alone. However, South Asian males specifically may not be considered underrepresented in STEM at HMC. **Hrishiv should verify eligibility carefully at hmc.edu/admission/fast/** before applying — the eligibility criteria may have evolved. HMC is a small school that actively tracks demographic representation in its student body, and the admissions office is accessible for direct questions. This is a case where emailing admission@hmc.edu to ask directly about eligibility for a South Asian male applicant is a reasonable approach.

## Requirements

Typical application components include:
- Short personal statement or essay responses
- Unofficial transcript
- Activities and honors list
- Confirmation of junior year enrollment
- Parent/guardian information

## What to Highlight in Application

- **Published ML research (NC State)** — HMC values deep intellectual engagement; a published research paper as a 10th grader signals the kind of self-directed rigor HMC's culture produces
- **TARC 2nd nationally** — HMC's engineering program covers aerospace-adjacent design; national rocketry achievement resonates
- **VEX Robotics top 150 worldwide** — competitive technical excellence at the global level
- **Science Olympiad** — HMC students often have deep Science Olympiad backgrounds; this is a natural community connection
- **Duke BME research** — shows interest in applied biological sciences, which maps to HMC's Biology/CS interdisciplinary tracks
- Articulate why a small, intensely STEM-focused environment appeals to you specifically

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **October:** Check the FAST URL; verify eligibility; note that December 1 falls during peak EA/ED application season — budget time carefully
- **Early-to-mid November:** Draft essays; the December deadline should not be treated as far away given other college application deadlines in October/November
- **Late November:** Finalize and submit; aim for November 25 to avoid Thanksgiving crunch
- **By November 28:** Submit

## Notes

Harvey Mudd is small enough that the fly-in class is also small — typically 30–50 students. Selection rates are not high. However, the visit itself — if admitted — is often described by participants as the most intensely intellectual campus visit experience available at the undergraduate level. The campus culture is famously collaborative (no academic competition among students), which is a strong fit for someone coming from a team-oriented competition background (VEX, TARC). The December deadline means FAST can be applied to after most fall fly-in decisions are received, potentially reducing the total fly-in application burden if earlier programs are secured.
