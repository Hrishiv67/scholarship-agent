## Notes
This is a manufacturing pre-apprenticeship program offered through Wright State University–Lake Campus in partnership with Mercer County ESC. Students work 4 days/week at a manufacturing company while earning wages, attend class 1 day/week, and maintain regular high school coursework in mornings. Program includes career coaching, resume building, interview preparation, and CMfgA exam preparation. Student is located in North Carolina (Cary, NC) but program appears to be Ohio-based (Celina, OH). Geographic eligibility unclear but likely requires proximity to Mercer County, Ohio area.
