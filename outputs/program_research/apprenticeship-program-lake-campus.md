## Notes
Manufacturing Pre-Apprenticeship Program offered through Wright State University–Lake Campus in partnership with Mercer County ESC and local manufacturers. Students earn wages while working 4 days/week with employer and attend classes 1 day/week. Students maintain regular high school coursework in mornings. Located in Celina, OH. Program examples show primarily Ohio high school participants (Parkway, New Knoxville, St. Henry). Student is from North Carolina (Green Hope High School, Cary NC) which may affect eligibility—geographic restriction unclear from page.
