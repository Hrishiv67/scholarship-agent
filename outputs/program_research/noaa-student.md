## Overview

NOAA runs several distinct student programs, not one unified application. The most relevant for Hrishiv are: (1) **NOAA Hollings Scholarship** (college-level, not applicable yet), (2) **NOAA Student Research Opportunity** via NESDIS and NWS offices — some accept HS students directly, (3) **Sea Grant internships** hosted at coastal universities, and (4) **NOAA Pathways Intern Program** (federal hiring pipeline, requires enrollment). The most accessible HS route is through NOAA-affiliated university partnerships — specifically, the Southeast Climate Science Center and NC State's NOAA-funded research groups already known to Hrishiv.
