**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

## Overview

The AFRL Scholars Program, administered by USRA (Universities Space Research Association), places high school and college students at Air Force Research Laboratory sites across the US — including Wright-Patterson AFB (OH), Kirtland AFB (NM), Edwards AFB (CA), and others. Students work alongside Air Force scientists and engineers on real research projects in areas like aerospace, directed energy, sensors, information systems, and propulsion. It is one of the few federally funded HS internships with a military R&D focus and a paid stipend.

## Requirements

- US citizenship (mandatory — security clearance environment)
- Transcript (unofficial acceptable at application stage)
- Two letters of recommendation (teacher or mentor preferred)
- Resume with coursework, extracurriculars, research experience
- Personal statement describing STEM interests and goals
- Minimum GPA ~3.0 (competitive applicants typically 3.5+)

## Notes

AFRL has multiple research directorates; when applying, you can indicate preferred areas. Target: Aerospace Systems Directorate (Wright-Patterson) or Space Vehicles Directorate (Kirtland). The application portal is run by USRA, not directly by the Air Force — check afrlscholars.usra.edu, not the AFRL main site. Deadline is notably earlier than most summer programs, so set a reminder for October.
