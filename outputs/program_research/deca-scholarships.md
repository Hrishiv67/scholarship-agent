## Overview

DECA (an association of marketing and business students) offers multiple scholarships each year for HS seniors who are active members. Several scholarships at different levels, some sponsored by corporate partners.

## Requirements

- Active DECA chapter membership (you qualify as NC DECA member)
- HS senior status
- Transcript
- Short essay about business/entrepreneurship/DECA experience
- Some scholarships may require financial need documentation
- ICDC or state competition participation is a plus (or required for some awards)

## Essay Approach

Your essay should cover:
- DECA competition performance (ICDC qualifier is strong)
- Business concepts or marketing skills developed through DECA
- Your pingpod.blog web project can be framed as entrepreneurship
- Career goals in business, marketing, technology, or entrepreneurship

## Your Competitive Profile

ICDC qualification (international level) puts you in the top tier of DECA competitors. Most applicants are state-level participants. This is a meaningful differentiator for DECA-specific scholarships.
