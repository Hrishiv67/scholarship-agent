## EASY WITH EXISTING WORK

Your Zenodo-published research paper (NC State hurricane tracking ML or Duke BME) **is** the research paper required for STS. No additional original research needed. The application adds:
- Short essays (reuse leadership/community service narrative)
- Teacher recommendations (existing relationships)
- Transcript + test scores

ROI: This is the highest-prestige competition you can enter with work you already have.

---

## Requirements

- Original research paper (independent research — your Zenodo publication qualifies)
- 3 short-answer essays (~300 words each)
- Up to 3 teacher/mentor recommendations
- Official transcript
- SAT/ACT scores (optional but helpful)
- One adult sponsor (teacher, mentor, or parent)

## Typical Essay Questions (vary by year — check portal when it opens)

Prior years have asked:
1. Describe your research project and its significance — what did you discover? What are the implications?
2. Describe a challenge you faced (scientific or personal) and how you overcame it
3. How has your research changed the way you think about a scientific question?
4. Describe your community impact or leadership role
5. What scientific question would you most like to answer in your lifetime?

Check societyforscience.org in September for the exact 2027 prompts.

## What a Strong Application Looks Like

- A clear, well-written research paper with original data analysis (not a review paper)
- Published or submitted to a journal is a major plus — Zenodo preprint counts
- Research in a hot area: ML/AI, climate/atmospheric science, biomedical engineering all score well
- Evidence of the applicant doing the actual science, not just assisting a professor
- Essays that show scientific curiosity beyond just the project — broad reading, follow-up questions
- Strong teacher recommendations that speak to intellectual independence
- National competition wins (rocketry 2nd nationally is directly relevant)
