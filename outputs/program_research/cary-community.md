## Overview

The Town of Cary administers several community recognition awards and scholarships through its Parks, Recreation, and Cultural Resources department. These include the Cary Outstanding Youth Award and related scholarship programs. The awards emphasize community service, leadership, and civic engagement — not purely academic merit. The programs are locally administered with a Cary-specific competition pool, meaning lower overall competition than national scholarships. Awards are modest but carry local recognition value and can be featured in college applications.

## Requirements

- Proof of Cary residency
- Transcript or academic record
- Essay describing community service contributions and leadership
- Letters of recommendation from community members, teachers, or nonprofit supervisors
- Documentation of community service hours and activities
