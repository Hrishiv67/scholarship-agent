## Overview

NC Governor's School is one of North Carolina's oldest and most prestigious gifted education programs, operating since 1963. Students attend a 4-week residential academic and arts program focusing on interdisciplinary learning rather than traditional coursework. There are subject areas including Natural Science, Mathematics, Social Science, Humanities, English, Choral Music, Instrumental Music, Theater Arts, and Visual Arts. The Natural Science and Mathematics areas are most relevant for Hrishiv. Students are expected to engage with philosophy of science, interdisciplinary ideas, and creative problem-solving — not just technical content.

## Requirements

- Nomination by Green Hope HS guidance counselor (you cannot self-nominate)
- School application packet including essay, teacher recommendations, and academic record
- Each school has a limited quota of nominations per subject area
- Governor's School selection committee reviews all nominated students statewide
