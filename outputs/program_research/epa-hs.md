## Overview

The Environmental Protection Agency runs student internship programs through two main channels: (1) the **EPA Internship Program** (formally through ORISE or USAJOBS for college students, some HS tracks), and (2) **EPA Region offices** which occasionally host local HS interns directly. EPA Region 4 covers North Carolina and is headquartered in Atlanta — but regional offices sometimes place interns at field locations. EPA's research arms (National Health and Environmental Effects Research Laboratory in RTP, NC — literally in Research Triangle Park) are worth investigating as a local placement option. The NHEERL/ORD in RTP is walking distance from RTI International.

## Requirements

- Application through EPA's USAJOBS postings or ORISE fellowship portal (orise.orau.gov)
- Transcript
- Personal statement describing environmental science interest
- Some programs require US citizenship
- HS-specific programs are sporadic — check USAJOBS for "High School" tag and EPA as agency
