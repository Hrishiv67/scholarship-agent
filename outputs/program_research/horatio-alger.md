# Horatio Alger National Scholarship

**URL:** https://scholars.horatioalger.org/scholarships/about-our-scholarship-programs/  
**Application Opens:** ~September 2026 (estimated — check site in August)  
**Deadline:** ~October 25, 2026 (estimated — early fall deadline)  
**Award:** $25,000 scholarship (National Scholars); $10,000 (State Scholars)  
**Tier:** Competitive  
**Eligibility:** HS seniors; US citizen; demonstrated adversity + financial need; 2.0+ GPA minimum (excellence in character, not grades)  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

The Horatio Alger Association awards scholarships to students who have overcome adversity and demonstrate strong character, community involvement, and commitment to higher education. This is explicitly NOT a merit/GPA scholarship — it's about resilience, character, and community service.

---

## Financial Need Requirement

This scholarship requires demonstrated financial need. Before applying, confirm your family's financial situation qualifies. There is no hard income cutoff, but applicants typically come from households earning under $55,000/year for the national scholarship. Check the current eligibility guidelines at horatioalger.org.

## Adversity Narrative

Your application needs a compelling adversity narrative. Consider:
- Challenges navigating being first-generation or immigrant-family background
- Any family hardships that required you to demonstrate resilience
- Cultural pressure and expectations as a South Asian student navigating your own path

Be genuine — the Horatio Alger Foundation is skilled at identifying authentic vs. crafted adversity narratives.

## Community Service Strength

Your Books for Africa project ($85,000 worth of books shipped to Kenya and Tanzania) is an exceptional community service story for this application. This is the kind of impact — measurable, international, humanitarian — that this scholarship recognizes.

## Requirements

- Online application at horatioalger.org
- Financial need documentation
- Short essays about adversity, community service, and goals
- Teacher/counselor recommendation
- Transcript (2.0+ GPA required — bar is low intentionally)
- Parental financial information

## What a Strong Application Looks Like

- Authentic adversity that you genuinely overcame
- Community service with documented, specific impact (Books for Africa is excellent)
- Character and integrity: what do you stand for?
- Clear goals for college and beyond
- Essays written in your own voice, not polished to sound like other scholarship essays

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **August–September 2026:** Check horatioalger.org for application opening; review financial eligibility
- **September–October 2026:** Draft essays; gather financial documentation
- **October 2026:** Submit by deadline (~October 25)

## Notes

If family income is above the informal threshold, this may not be a strong fit. Focus application time on other scholarships. If financial eligibility is met, this is a very strong application given your community service profile.
