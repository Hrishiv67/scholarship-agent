# NASA High School Internship (OSSI)

**URL:** https://intern.nasa.gov/  
**Application Opens:** ~October 2026 (estimated)  
**Deadline:** Rolling — apply as soon as portal opens; many positions fill by February  
**Award:** Paid; $500–$800/week depending on center and project; 10 weeks  
**Tier:** Competitive  
**Eligibility:** HS students 16+; US citizen; 3.0+ GPA  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

NASA's OSSI (One Stop Shopping Initiative) portal aggregates internship opportunities across all NASA centers. HS track available. You apply to specific opportunities (lab + project) rather than a single program. Apply to 5-10 different opportunities for best chance.

---

## Best-Fit NASA Centers for Your Profile

- **NASA Langley Research Center (Hampton, VA):** Aeronautics, atmospheric science, ML applications — 6 hours from Cary
- **NASA Goddard Space Flight Center (Greenbelt, MD):** Atmospheric science, Earth observation, satellite data
- **NASA Marshall Space Flight Center (Huntsville, AL):** Propulsion, rocketry, aerospace — direct TARC fit
- **NASA Ames Research Center (Mountain View, CA):** ML, AI, computer science

Of these, Langley (~6 hours) or Goddard (~6-7 hours) are closest to Cary. NASA generally does not provide housing.

## Requirements

- Profile on intern.nasa.gov
- Transcript (GPA 3.0+)
- Personal statement per application
- US citizenship
- 16+ years old at internship start
- Some opportunities require faculty/teacher recommendation

## Application Strategy

1. Create intern.nasa.gov profile as soon as portal opens in fall
2. Set up email alerts for new HS opportunities
3. Apply to every relevant opportunity immediately — don't wait
4. Customize each personal statement to the specific opportunity/center
5. Mention hurricane ML research for atmospheric science centers; TARC for propulsion/aerospace

## Your Competitive Profile

**Strengths:** Hurricane ML research = direct fit for atmospheric science centers; TARC 2nd nationally = direct fit for propulsion/rocketry centers; Python + ML = relevant everywhere.

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **October 2026:** Create intern.nasa.gov profile; browse listed opportunities
- **November–December 2026:** Apply to 5-8 specific opportunities with customized statements
- **January 2027:** Apply to any remaining opportunities before they fill
- **March–April 2027:** NASA contacts candidates

## Notes

NASA does not provide housing or travel — budget for travel and housing near the center. This is a significant cost for out-of-state centers. NIEHS (NIH in Research Triangle Park) may be more practical for NC-based options. However, a NASA internship is uniquely prestigious and worth the logistics effort.
