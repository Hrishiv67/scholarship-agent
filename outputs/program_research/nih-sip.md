# NIH Summer Internship Program (SIP)

**URL:** https://www.training.nih.gov/programs/sip  
**Application Portal:** https://irta.nih.gov/  
**Application Opens:** ~November 2026 (estimated)  
**Deadline:** March 1, 2027 (✅ consistent deadline every year)  
**Award:** Paid stipend ~$500–$900/week depending on lab and experience; 8–10 weeks; Bethesda MD  
**Tier:** Competitive  
**Eligibility:** HS students who are 16+ years old; US citizen or permanent resident; 3.0+ GPA  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

NIH (National Institutes of Health) offers summer research positions at its main campus in Bethesda, Maryland and other locations. High school students work directly in research labs alongside scientists. This is one of the most accessible paid federal research internships for HS students.

---

## Requirements

- Application submitted through irta.nih.gov (not NIH main site)
- Unofficial transcript (GPA 3.0+ required)
- Brief personal statement / statement of interest
- At least 16 years old by program start
- US citizen or permanent resident
- No teacher recommendation required (some labs may request one)

## Application Process

1. Create account at irta.nih.gov
2. Browse available HS positions by research area (filter to "High School")
3. Apply to specific labs that interest you — each lab has its own listing
4. Lab supervisor reviews and contacts you if interested
5. Accept a match; complete onboarding

**Pro tip:** Apply to 5-10 different labs in your areas of interest (computational biology, biomedical engineering, atmospheric science, physics). More applications = more chances.

## Research Areas That Fit Your Profile

- **National Cancer Institute (NCI):** Computational biology, ML in cancer research
- **National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (NHLBI):** Biomedical + engineering
- **National Institute of Biomedical Imaging and Bioengineering (NIBIB):** Direct fit for Duke BME work
- **National Library of Medicine (NLM):** Bioinformatics, ML applications
- **National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences (NIEHS):** Research Triangle Park, NC location — no DC move required

## What a Strong Application Looks Like

- Specific statement of interest mentioning the lab's work and why you're a fit
- Evidence of independent research (Zenodo paper = huge advantage)
- Relevant skills: Python, MATLAB, data analysis, lab techniques
- Good grades in science/math
- Applying to multiple labs in your area of interest

## Your Competitive Profile

**Strengths:**
- Published research (Zenodo) is rare for HS applicants — immediately distinguishes you
- Python + ML skills = directly applicable to computational biology/bioinformatics labs
- Duke BME scaffold research = directly relevant to NIBIB track
- 3.0+ GPA easily met

## Logistics

- Bethesda MD campus is ~5 hours from Cary NC (too far for commuting — need housing)
- NIH does NOT provide housing; many interns use university housing near campus or private rentals
- NIEHS in Research Triangle Park is commutable from Cary — prioritize those listings
- Stipend covers food and some housing but not all

## Recommended Prep Timeline

- **October–November 2026:** Browse irta.nih.gov to see prior-year lab listings and research areas
- **November 2026:** Application opens — create account immediately
- **December 2026 – February 2027:** Apply to 8-10 labs; customize each statement of interest
- **March 1, 2027:** Hard deadline — submit by then
- **March–April 2027:** Labs contact candidates for matches

## Notes

The March 1 deadline is hard and consistent. Missing it means waiting a full year. This is a high-value, accessible program — the application is not very competitive compared to RSI or Clark Scholars. Strong STEM students who apply thoughtfully are frequently accepted.
