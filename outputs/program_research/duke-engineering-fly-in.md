# Duke Engineering Diversity Fly-In

**URL:** https://pratt.duke.edu/undergrad/apply/diversity
**Application Opens:** November to December (estimated)
**Deadline:** ~January 15 (estimated; verify at URL above)
**Award:** All-expenses fly-in to Duke's Pratt School of Engineering in Durham, NC
**Tier:** Competitive
**Eligibility:** Underrepresented high school juniors interested in engineering; Duke Pratt typically includes underrepresented racial/ethnic minorities, first-generation college students, and students with financial need
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

---

## Overview

Duke's Pratt School of Engineering hosts a dedicated diversity fly-in for high school juniors who are considering engineering. The program typically runs for two days and includes lab tours, faculty panels, engineering design challenges, residential life sessions, and financial aid presentations. Duke is a top-10 research university with an outstanding engineering school — BME and CS are particular strengths. Duke BME is consistently ranked #1 or #2 in the country at the undergraduate level, and the CS program has grown substantially with strong AI and data science offerings. Duke's location in Durham, NC makes this uniquely accessible for Hrishiv — it is approximately 30 miles from Cary, making it feasible to attend even without the all-expenses component.

## Eligibility Note — South Asian Applicants

Duke Pratt's diversity fly-in has historically focused on students from underrepresented racial/ethnic groups (Black, Hispanic/Latino, Native American, Pacific Islander) and first-generation college students. South Asian students are generally **not** categorized as underrepresented in engineering at Duke. **Hrishiv must verify eligibility on the Pratt diversity page before applying.** However, this program is worth a particularly close look because Hrishiv already has a relationship with Duke through his Duke BME research — it may be worth contacting his Duke research supervisor or mentor to ask for guidance on whether the fly-in is appropriate, or whether they can provide an introductory email to the Pratt admissions office.

## Requirements

Typical application components include:
- Short essays on interest in engineering and personal background
- Unofficial transcript
- Activities and honors list
- Current junior enrollment confirmation
- Letter of recommendation from a teacher or counselor (sometimes required for engineering fly-ins)
